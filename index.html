Hi, I’m Pascal Amiet.

I am a PhD student in Economics at the Institute of Economics and Econometrics (IEE) at the University of Geneva. I am supervised by Aleksey Tetenov and Christophe Gaillac.

My research interests span econometrics, machine learning, and statistics, with a focus on policy learning and treatment-effect inference. I am also interested in applications to political economy.

I hold a Master's degree from the University of Zurich. Before joining the IEE I was working as a research assistant at the University of St. Gallen. More information can be found in my CV.

Undergraduate · 2025-present

Econometrics

The aim of this course is to present the core models and methods in econometrics.

Course overview

At the end of this course the student should be able to: specify a suitable econometric model to answer an empirical research question; devise and motivate an appropriate estimation methodology; test the assumptions of the model; eventually make predictions using the model; make evidence-based policy recommendations. Overall, the student should have a strong foundation in econometrics to learn further.

Graduate · Upcoming

Applied Economics Workshop

The aim of the workshop is to have students familiarize with the current methods of empirical analysis of microdata for policy relevant topics.

Course overview

The main focus will be on hands-on, and interactive, understanding of frontier research in applied economics. In particular, we will cover a series of topics, firstly from a theoretical perspective and then with applications and replications.
